## Required .env vars

In the `register` or `boot` method of AppServiceProvider
This will make sure the .env var is both present and not empty

```php
$dotenv = Dotenv::createImmutable($this->app->environmentPath());
$dotenv->required('TEST_ENV_VAR')->notEmpty();
```
