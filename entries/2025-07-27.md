# Optimizing for large data processing 
query only the data you need
cache queries

Extracting with all relationships (~57k records) took just under 10 mins with the current version to pull products from EBMS and save them in local db for processing

The whole process from extracting to transforming and saving to db too ~12 minutes 

Just the transforming and saving took about just under 2mins without caching queries
